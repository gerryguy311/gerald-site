Intro Script

Full introduction

About 45 seconds

Every weekday morning at 8am Eastern, a few hundred people log on to hear one person walk through what happened in cybersecurity in the last twenty four hours, and what it actually means for their job.

That has now happened 1,204 weekday mornings in a row.

Not a campaign. Not a product launch. Just showing up, every single day, for years. And somewhere along the way it stopped being a show and became a community. Seventeen thousand people in a Discord server. A couple hundred thousand more on YouTube. And a long list of practitioners who will tell you they got their first job in this field because of something that started on that livestream.

He has a PhD in cyber operations, two decades in the industry, and a SANS award for the show. Those are real, but they're not why he's up here.

He's up here because he answered a question most people only ask themselves once: what actually happens if you just don't stop?

Please welcome Dr. Gerald Auger.
